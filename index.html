Sourcing & Heritage

Heirloom Spices & Sustainable Wood-Fire

At Saffron & Smoke, every ingredient is ethically sourced from partner farms. We roast whole coriander, green cardamom, star anise, and saffron threads in-house each morning to preserve essential oils. Our live grills burn sustainably harvested white oak and fruitwood, imparting a clean, sweet smoke profile without artificial flavorings.
